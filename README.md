# Goal Stream

Goal Stream is a platform designed to help individuals and teams set, track, and achieve their goals efficiently. By allowing users to break down their objectives into actionable steps, visualize progress, and collaborate with others, Goal Stream empowers you to stay motivated and organized on your journey to success.

## Features

- **Goal Setting:** Create personal or team goals, set deadlines, and define key milestones.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor progress visually with charts and timelines.
- **Step Breakdown:** Split your goal into tasks and subtasks for detailed planning.
- **Collaboration:** Work with teammates, assign tasks, and share updates.
- **Notifications:** Receive reminders for upcoming deadlines and important updates.
